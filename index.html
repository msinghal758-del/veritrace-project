How VeriTrace works

Three things every response team needs under pressure: a clear next step, proof of what was done, and a report ready before the deadline.

Know the next step

Guidance for each of the four NIST incident phases, with every action linked to the MITRE ATT&CK technique it answers and the playbook it came from.

Prove what happened

Every action, file and approval is time-stamped and chained to the entry before it with a SHA-256 hash, so any later change is obvious to an auditor.

Report on time

A compliance-ready draft of the incident report, listing the notifications you owe, who they go to and when each one is due.

Every deadline, visible from the first minute

Banks in India have six hours to report a cyber incident, and seventy-two hours when personal data is involved. VeriTrace starts the right clocks the moment you log an incident.

0 hDiscovery
6 hCERT-In and RBI
72 hGDPR and DPDP
CERT-In, 6 hoursDirections of April 2022, for any reportable cyber incident.
RBI, 6 hoursCyber incidents at regulated banks and financial entities.
GDPR, 72 hoursArticle 33, when EU residents' personal data is breached.
DPDP, 72 hoursRule 7 detailed report to the Data Protection Board.
